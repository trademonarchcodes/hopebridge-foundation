Our Causes

Human needs are connected. So is our commitment to care.

AREAS OF FOCUS

Six pathways to meaningful support

These are the foundation's proposed areas of focus. Specific programme activities will be published as they are confirmed.

FOCUS AREA 01

Education for All

Learning resources, school support, scholarships and access to educational opportunities.

FOCUS AREA 02

Food & Basic Needs

Food, clothing, shelter and other essentials that help people meet immediate needs.

FOCUS AREA 03

Healthcare for Humanity

Medical assistance, treatment support and initiatives that promote wellbeing.

FOCUS AREA 04

Vulnerable Lives Support

Respectful support for children, widows, older people and people living with disabilities.

FOCUS AREA 05

Emergency Humanitarian Response

Relief-oriented support for people and communities affected by emergencies and crises.

FOCUS AREA 06

Empowerment & Development

Skills, youth development, livelihoods and pathways toward stronger communities.
